# Kijani Market: Farm-to-Client App PRD

## Overview
A mobile application connecting local farmers directly to customers. The design follows a "Pinterest-inspired" aesthetic with staggered grids, rounded corners, and a warm, earthy color palette.

## Core User Journeys
1. **Onboarding & Auth**: Secure login/signup with JWT management.
2. **Browsing**: Staggered product grid with category filtering and search.
3. **Purchasing**: Cart management, delivery address pinning, and M-Pesa integration.
4. **Fulfillment**: Order history for customers and a task list for drivers.

## Visual Direction
- **Style**: Pinterest-inspired (staggered grids, high border radius).
- **Theme**: Earthy tones (Greens, browns, creams).
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Roboto).
- **Components**: Material 3 base with custom card elevations.

## Screen List
1. **Splash/Onboarding**: Brand introduction.
2. **Auth (Login/Signup)**: Earthy, clean forms.
3. **Home/Catalog**: Staggered grid of fresh produce.
4. **Product Detail**: High-quality imagery and "Verified Purchase" reviews.
5. **Cart**: List of items with Room-backed persistence.
6. **Checkout**: Address selection and payment polling.
7. **Order Status**: Stepper showing delivery progress.
8. **Driver Dashboard**: Simplified task list for fulfillment.
